# Data source and limitations

Source: Australian Government Department of Infrastructure, Transport, Regional Development, Communications, Sport and the Arts, National Road Safety Data Hub — Monthly road deaths. Data through June 2026; source page last updated 15 July 2026.

The prototype stores a curated aggregate snapshot of published tables for teaching/demo purposes. It does not include individual-level ARDD records. Recent source figures are preliminary and may be revised. Totals may include unknown categories, and remoteness reporting should be interpreted cautiously.
